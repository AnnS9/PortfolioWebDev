Aesthetics beauty website for local business

Project Overview

Aesthetics by Natalia is a professional web platform developed for a local beauty and skincare business. The site functions as a clean digital storefront that presents treatment offerings, service details, and essential business information to prospective clients.

Key Features

  • WordPress and Elementor Build: Built using WordPress for backend content management and Elementor for flexible page design, creating a tailored layout matching the brand vision.
  • Custom Visual Branding: Applied a specific color palette requested by the business owner to maintain aesthetic consistency across all pages.
  • Content and Media Integration: Incorporated text descriptions and photography supplied by the client to highlight treatments accurately.
  • Booking and Social Integration: Connected external scheduling tools to handle client appointments efficiently alongside links to active social media channels to support online engagement.

Tools and Technologies

  • Platform: WordPress
  • Page Builder: Elementor
  • Integrations: External booking platform, social media channel links
  • Asset Management: Client-provided photography and text copy
